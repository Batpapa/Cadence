Description

Cadence is a free, open-source flashcard application. It runs entirely in your browser and stores data locally. A few optional features talk to a server: Google Drive sync (to back up and synchronise your data across devices), a short-lived share relay operated by the author (to hand a card pack or session to someone else via a one-time key), and lookups against TheSession.org / IrishTuneInfo.com when importing tunes. None of these servers keep a durable copy of your data — see the Privacy Policy for the details of what each one sees and for how long.
