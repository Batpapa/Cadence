Description

Cadence is a free, open-source flashcard application, available on the web and as an app for Android. It runs on your device and stores your data there. A few optional features talk to a server: Google Drive sync (to back up and synchronise your data across devices), a short-lived share relay operated by the author (to hand a card pack or a tune analysis to someone else via a one-time key), lookups against TheSession.org and, if you turn it on, irishtune.info, downloads of public data (tune popularity, tune names, the recognition index), and the services behind the links you add to your cards. None of these servers keep a durable copy of your data — see the Privacy Policy for the details of what each one sees and for how long.

Tune recognition, the boundaries placed on a recording, audio sync, play-along and tempo come from automatic analysis of sound. They can be wrong — a tune missed, a tune mistaken for another, a score following the wrong place — and are offered as help, not as a measure of anyone's playing.

The Google Drive sync feature is entirely optional. When enabled, Cadence stores a data file (cadence-data.json) and a folder of larger files (cadence-data-ext) in your Google Drive, using the drive.file scope.
